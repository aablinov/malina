## 1. What it is

A separate mode on a map with `meta.td` (`src/td/types.ts`): in the centre — the **raspberry bush**; waves of the Order's enemies march to it from the edges along **roads**; the player builds **towers** on **pads** along the roads for raspberries and fights with the sword between them. Lost — the bush withered (health 0); won — all waves repelled. The hero meanwhile lives by the usual playground rules: click-to-walk, sword, bow, spells, dodge roll, potion.

Stages (one commit per stage): 1 building mechanic → 2 five kinds of towers → 3 bush and roads → 4 waves and new enemies → 5 spawning → 6 lava map → map 2 (glade) → map 3 (castle siege).

Open: `dist/index.html?map=td` (`npm run map:td` rebuilds `assets/maps/td.json` from `src/level/td-map.ts`).

## 2. Building towers (stage 1 — done)

- **Pad** (`TdPad`, `meta.td.pads`) — a circle of slabs with a cobblestone rim on the map and a golden highlight ring in the scene (`Pad` in `src/td/td.ts`): dim, brighter under the cursor, almost out under a tower.
- **How to open the menu:** walk up (2 m from the edge) and press **F**, or **click the pad** — the hero walks to its edge and the menu opens by itself (the reticle over a pad is a golden square). Walked farther than 3 m — the menu closes. **Esc / F** — close.
- **Menu** (`.td-menu`, at the bottom above the HUD bar): five tower cards — key **1–5**, colour, name, what it fires, range, price; not enough raspberries — the card is dimmed. On an occupied pad — **1 upgrade** (to II, to III) and **2 sell**.
- **Treasury — the profile's raspberries** (`profile.data.berries`): at map start it becomes `meta.td.berries` (300), building and upgrading subtract, selling returns 70 % of what was invested (`SELL_BACK`), raspberries drop from fallen enemies in piles — pick them up as always. **N** — restart the defence: towers demolished, enemies removed, treasury full.
- **Tower** (`Tower`, `src/td/tower.ts`): a model of little cubes on a stone octagonal base, the rotating part faces the target; levels I–III — damage ×1 / ×1.6 / ×2.4, range +0 / +1 / +2 m, upgrade price 70 % and 100 % of the build price; the level mark — raspberry lamp-berries on the corners of the base (one, two). Target — **whoever has gone farthest along the road** among those in range; keeps the target while it is alive and in range. Damage goes into `Target.hit`, like from the sword: mobs flash, crumble, drop raspberries. A tower is an obstacle for the hero, pathing and enemies.
- In the playground: the hero's `targets()` sees the mode's enemies, `solids()` and mob obstacles — towers; `__malina.td` — `pads`, `towers`, `build(pad, kind)`, `upgrade(pad)`, `sell(pad)`, `spawn(kind, at)`, `pack`. Test — `tests/td.spec.ts`.

## 3. Kinds of towers (stage 2 — done)

Numbers — `src/td/towers.ts`, models and shots — `src/td/tower.ts`. Each has a rotating part facing the target and a living detail: crossbow recoil, catapult arm, brazier sparks, crystal sway, a hovering rune.

| Tower | Price | Range | Reload | Damage | What it fires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Archer tower | 60 | 9 m | 0.4 s | 6 | a bolt at one target, fast |
| Frost spire | 80 | 8 m | 1.1 s | 8 | an ice clot: slows by −45 % for 2 s; III — area frost |
| Catapult | 110 | 11 m | 2.4 s | 26 | a stone in an arc, explosion of radius 2.4 m with knockback |
| Fire brazier | 100 | 6 m | 0.9 s | 10 | a cone jet of flame at everyone ahead, ignites (3 more ticks) |
| Arcane obelisk | 130 | 10 m | 1.5 s | 18 | an orb flies to the target, hits and weakens (damage to it ×1.3), chains to neighbours |

- **Archer tower** (`arrow`): four posts, a platform with railings, a crossbow on a swivel; the bolt flies straight at 34 m/s; II — a canopy, III — a pennant.
- **Frost spire** (`frost`): a pedestal, a tilted ice crystal of prisms (taller with level), shards in a circle, frost; the clot at 22 m/s: damage and `chill(0.45, 2.2 s)`; at III — frost 2 m around the target (half damage, slow) and a ring.
- **Catapult** (`splash`): a frame, uprights, an arm with a bucket and counterweight (II — bigger, III — a pennant), a stock of stones; the stone flies in an arc (0.6 s + 0.05 s/m), an explosion of radius 2.4 m — damage from 100 % at the centre to 40 % at the edge, knockback; turns slowly (3 rad/s).
- **Fire brazier** (`fire`): an iron bowl with a spout on stone legs, embers and flame (taller with level), sparks always; the jet — a cone of ±0.42 rad to full range at everyone at once, each burns for 3 s at 30 % damage per second (`tower.burning`).
- **Arcane obelisk** (`magic`): a dark carved pillar, a rune hovering above it (satellites with level); a ribbon orb (`fx.streak`) in an arc to the target, tracking it; on hit — `expose(0.3, 3 s)` and damage, then chains to neighbours within 4 m (one per level, 60 % damage, lightning between them).

## 4. Bush and roads (stage 3 — done)

- **Raspberry bush** (`RaspberryBush`, `src/td/bush.ts`): a stump with roots on a mound, three tiers of foliage, clusters of raspberry lamps and a heart-berry on top. Health — `meta.td.bush.hp` (400). To mobs it is a **decoy** (`src/decoys.ts`) with `taunt` 7 m: hits from those who reach it go into `hurt` — a flash, a number, leaves fly; the heart beats faster and dimmer with damage, berries go out, below half dry leaves fall. The trunk is an obstacle (the crown is not, so mobs can reach). Health 0 — **the bush withered**: the crown shrivels and browns, `state = 'lost'`, a banner, **N** — restart.
- **Roads** (`meta.td.lanes`, `TdLane`): points from a lair to the bush. `td.spawnOn(kind, laneId)` places a mob at the first point and gives it `march` — a one-way route (`src/mobs/mob.ts`): it goes at full speed (×0.85) through the points, at the end — alarm, and the bush decoy becomes the target like a hero (attacks, markers and hits are the same). `progress` — the fraction of the road travelled: towers hit whoever has the most.
- **Hero and the march:** a marching mob notices the hero only within **3.5 m** (`aggro`); a sword hit (`notice(byHit)`) — only if the hero is within `aggro + 2`, tower shots from afar do not distract; a struck mob chases the hero, but once it has run farther than the **leash** of 10 m (`leash`) with no decoy around — back to marching. The hero fell — the march continues. A decoy with `taunt` is visible to a mob from the taunt radius even when that is larger than its `aggro`.
- F1 panel: an "On the road: …" button for every road — a random enemy of the five. HUD: the bush health bar under the mode line.

## 5. Waves and enemies (stage 4 — done)

**Waves** — `meta.td.waves` (`TdWave`, the list in `src/td/waves.ts`, ten of them): groups `{ kind, count, every, lane, delay }` emerge from the lair of their road every `every` seconds after `delay` from the wave start; the reward `bounty` goes into the treasury when all have emerged and all have fallen. Between waves — a rest `meta.td.rest` (20 s); **G** (or the button in F1) — the wave now, the bonus — raspberries for every second not waited. Enemies toughen with the waves: pack level 1, 4, 7, … — `waveLevel` in `src/td/scaling.ts` (`pack.setScale`, health and damage as for wardens: the tenth wave — level 28, health × 2.6, damage × 1.8). All repelled — `state = 'won'`, banner "The bush held!". HUD: "Wave N of M", how many enemies are on the map or how long until the next and the bonus. Waves: scouting (ten raspberry runts) → couriers → gardeners → wolves and slimes → moths → two battering rams with squires → piglets and beetles → the guard → thorns → the last onslaught (six battering rams, moths, wolves, twenty couriers, squires and two thorn monsters). ≈ 420 enemies solo in total.

**Scaling by player count** (`td.players`, `td.setPlayers`; in the playground — "Players" in F1, the same selector as for wardens): enemies in each group `⌈count · (1 + 0.5·(players − 1))⌉` — double for three, 2.5× for four; each one's health × the same multiplier again (via `pack.setScale`, damage to players does not grow); the wave reward × the same multiplier — the treasury is shared, more towers are needed. In multiplayer everyone has their own raspberries ([design-multiplayer.md](design-multiplayer.md), section 7): the wave reward to each — without the multiplier. The HUD shows "players N" when there is more than one. The wave numbers in `waves.ts` are for one player.

**Scaling by the heroes' strength** (section 16): the pack level of every wave + (rating − 1), rating = gear power + ⌊(level − 1)/2⌋, a party — the average.

**New enemies** (`src/mobs/td-foes.ts`, nos. 32–34 in the registry, absent from the showcase buttons — `TD_FOES`):

| No. | Enemy | Health | Speed | What it does | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 32 | Berry courier (`runner`) | 14 | 5.5 m/s | a berry with a sack on long legs, slips past slow towers, nibbles the bush for 3 | archer tower and frost; the sword — one hit |
| 33 | Vine battering ram (`ram`) | 160 | 1.6 m/s | a boar of logs and vine, a bark shield on its brow: a hit against its facing ×0.35; at the bush headbutts for 14 with knockback | hit from behind and the sides: catapult by area, obelisk by chain, flame burns regardless of the shield |
| 34 | Pollen moth (`moth`) | 22 | 3.2 m/s | flies at a height of 1.3 m through fences and obstacles (`ghost`), pollen at the bush for 5 | fragile: frost and the archer tower; stunned, it descends |

## 6. Spawning (stage 5 — done)

- **Lair** (`Lair`, `src/td/lair.ts`) — at the first point of each road, in a cave of the map: a rune ring on the floor (raspberry-coloured, four rune strokes) and a vertical **veil** of light across the exit. `td.spawnOn` places the enemy at the ring with a scatter of ±0.6 m, it climbs out of the ground (the mob's `spawn` state), the lair gives a **flash, a ring wave, sparks and a hum** (`burst`, counter `spawned`).
- **Warning:** 6 s before a wave the lairs of its roads (`td.nextLanes`) flare up and pulse ever faster (`warn` 0 → 1), sparks rise above the ring; while the groups emerge — full alarm. HUD during rest: "next in N s — western path, northern path · G now (+bonus)".
- Enemies emerge from the wave's group queue (section 5): a group waits `delay`, then one every `every` seconds; the queue is empty and all have fallen — the wave is repelled.

## 7. Map: lava (stage 6 — done)

After the third reference — a lava night: dark warm ground, lava glowing in rifts with haze above, volcanoes in the corners, little fences, lanterns and braziers along the roads, cages and skulls by the lairs. Generator `src/level/td-map.ts` (`npm run map:td`), 96 × 96 m, the map whole in the scene (no regions).

- **Blocks** (`src/level/palette.ts`, category "lava"; textures in `textures.ts`): `ash_ground` — ashen ground (natural, the colour drifts in patches), `basalt` (+ half-block, stairs) — dark cobblestone, `cliff_lava` / `cliff_lava_top` — scorched rock in brown strata with ash on top, `lava` — a wadeable liquid (knee-deep) with its own mesher layer: slow waves, yellow foam at the shore, emission under bloom, **baked warm light** on the blocks around (`bakedLight`, 4 m — that is how the rift walls and bridges glow). The hero in lava **burns**: 6 damage every 0.4 s, sparks, hissing (`src/dev/playground.ts`, on any map with lava).
- **Rifts** (`RIFTS`): two long polylines across the whole map (north and south), four short ones and six pools (`POOLS`); the outline is ragged by noise, the shores — pieces of basalt and **lava flowers** (`flower_orange`, `flower_red`). Lava does not touch the bush, pads and lairs (`keep`).
- **Roads** — 3 m cobblestone with a ragged edge; across lava — a **bridge**: `bridge_deck` planking, beams, posts and a fence railing on the sides; along an ordinary road — `fence` pieces, as in the reference. The bush square — slabs in a ring of basalt with half-block merlons and four lanterns; pads — a circle of stone in a basalt rim with four little posts on the diagonals.
- **Volcanoes** (`volcano`): a cone with a ragged edge, height 3–6, the crater filled with lava and fire, a lava flow down the slope in a random direction. Four in the corners, two small. **Cliffs** (`lavaRock`) — eight massifs between the roads; the map edge — scorched rock 2–3 blocks. Lairs — caves in such rock, fire inside, torches at the entrance, a cage (`crate_banded`), a skull and bones.
- **Decor:** dead trees, boulders and stumps (library presets), lanterns and campfires at road bends, on the ground — dry grass, skulls, bones, pots, firewood, barrels, sacks, fire on basalt.
- **Haze over lava** (`LavaFx`, `src/td/lava-fx.ts`): for every 6 × 6 m containing lava — a soft orange plane (radial texture, additive) at a height of 1.45 m, breathing; from lava within 22 m of the hero rise sparks and ash flakes. Created on any map that lives whole in the scene and has a `lava` block.
- **World** (`meta.world`): a low orange sun (1.4), sky and haze the colour of embers (`#3a1c14`, 18–76 m), warm ambient, weather "sparks and ash", bloom 0.7, vignette 0.3, shadows with a violet tint.

## 8. Map 2: Crystal Glade (done)

The second map — `td2` (`src/level/td-glade-map.ts`, `npm run map:td2`, `?map=td2`), after reference no. 1 from [sources/td-refs.md](sources/td-refs.md): a night glade of 96 × 96 m, enemies **a level stronger** and three new kinds.

- **Moat roads.** Three roads with right angles, like the reference's moats: the western, northern and eastern moats lead from the edge to the bush square; on both sides — **stone walls** (`wall_stone_wall`, mossy brick, posts on every fifth block) with openings at the pads (4.5 m), at the square and **every 9 m** — the hero crosses the moat anywhere; the fence ring around the bush is open towards the nearby pads. Test `tests/td-pads.spec.ts`: on all three maps every pad is reachable by click (also when all pads are built up), and a pad on the ground by keys — from at least seven sides out of eight. Mobs walk only at y = 0, so the moat is not sunken — the walls draw it. Pads (17) — at the outer corners of the turns, a circle of slabs with a mossy rim and a small crystal; a check in code: a pad is no closer than 3.2 m to a road and not on the square, otherwise the export fails.
- **Portal lairs.** At the first point of each road — a stone arch 3 × 4 (`portal_frame`, `portal_trim`) with a **blue glow** `portal_blue` (a new block, the same wave layer `portal`, cold baked light) in a niche of dark stone; in front of it the portal floor, crystals and two hooded statues. The lair (`Lair`) draws its ring and veil there.
- **Light.** Night: a blue sun of 0.55, cold haze, bloom 0.85, fireflies — a new kind of weather `fireflies` (`src/weather.ts`, green sparks rising slowly). **Mushroom lamps** give light: blocks `shroom_stem` + `shroom_cap_pink` / `shroom_cap_blue` (+ half-blocks for wide caps) on the glow layer with baked pink/blue light over 4 m; small glowing mushrooms and crystals as decor, crystals as blocks at pads and portals, lanterns on the square. The ground — grass with patches of turquoise moss and moss, ferns, pink and blue flowers; in the corners, platforms with statues and altars with a crystal, along the edge — firs and oaks, a fence in moss.
- **Waves** — `src/td/waves-glade.ts`, twelve: glade scouting → sporelings → shellbacks → couriers and hoppers → wolves under spores → ram and shell → moths and spores → the glade guard → the shellback march → hoppers → thorns and spores → masters of the glade (four battering rams from two sides, eight shellbacks, ten sporelings, two thorn monsters, 24 couriers). Bush 500, treasury 350, `meta.td.level` = 7: the pack starts at level 7 and reaches 30 by the twelfth wave (health × 2.7 even before the player multiplier).
- **New enemies** (`src/mobs/td-foes.ts`, nos. 35–37, in `TD_FOES`):
  - **Crystal shellback** (`shellback`, 90 health, 2.2 m/s, mass 2) — a beetle with a cluster of crystals on its back: the shell **absorbs the first six hits entirely** (the number "shell", a crystal vanishes per hit, the sixth — "cracked" with a flash), after that full damage. Brazier burn ticks count as hits too, so fire and the catapult on a crowd strip the shell faster than arrows. At the bush — a full-body blow, 12.
  - **Sporeling** (`sporeling`, 40 health, 2.6 m/s) — a walking mushroom with a violet cap: neighbours within 3 m **heal 3 per second**, and when it bursts (from anything) — a cloud of spores heals everyone within 3.5 m **by 30 %**. Hit it first and away from the crowd; the `given` field — how much it has healed. At the bush — pollen, 5.
  - **Rainbow hopper** (`hopper`, 30 health, 3.4 m/s) — a hare-wisp: on the march every 3 s it **jumps 5 m forward along the road points** with a rainbow arc (`hops`), frost does not slow the jump; noticed by the hero, it stops jumping. At the bush — a kick, 6.
- Tests — `tests/td2.spec.ts`: the shell, the sporeling's healing and cloud, the hopper's jumps; the map — portals, walls, road walkability, pack level 7, fireflies.

## 9. Map 3: castle siege (done)

The third map — `td3` (`src/level/td-castle-map.ts`, `npm run map:td3`, `?map=td3`), after the references in [sources/castle-refs.md](sources/castle-refs.md) (the look of castles and the wall mechanics from BFME). The castle stands **on a meadow**, the players are inside behind the walls, the bush is in the centre of the courtyard; enemies approach in groups **from four sides**. The same `TowerDefense` mode, with walls added.

- **Castle.** A square of walls 39 × 39 m (`bricks`, mossy brick at the bottom) 3 blocks high: the outer row — merlons (`bricks` / `bricks_slab` alternating), the two inner rows — a **walkway on top** of slabs (y = 3), along which the hero walks from bastion to bastion. Four corner bastions 5 × 5, two gatehouse bastions in the north and six **small bastions** flanking the future breaches — all with pads (`TdPad.y` = 3). **Stairs** onto the wall — four flights of steps at the inner faces (`bricks_stairs`, a rise of 0.5 per cell, click pathing knows them). In the courtyard — four pads, a well, lanterns, crates; in the north the **gate**: an opening of 3 × 2 between the gatehouse towers, wooden leaves ajar inward — **the hero passes through the wicket, enemies do not** (the leaves are an obstacle only for mobs, `td.foeSolids`). Outside — the meadow: enemy paths, groves, boulders, ruins, flowers; at the edge of the roads the Order's camps (tents, a campfire, a palisade) with a lair. Light — a clear day, but the haze is dense (20–52 m from the camera), and the camps are hidden by **mist** (`meta.td.mist`, `src/td/mist-fx.ts`): around the start of each road within 13 m — layers of soft planes at three heights, denser towards the centre, drifting slowly; the emergence points are not visible from the courtyard or the walls — where a wave comes from is known only from the HUD and the lair alarm.
- **Walls and gate** (`meta.td.walls`, `TdWall`; class `Wall` in `src/td/wall.ts`) — decoys (`Decoy`) with health: three curtain walls of 400 each (eastern, southern, western) and the gate 250. An enemy road ends at a wall (`TdLane.wall`): reaching the end, the enemy "notices" the wall (taunt 6, at the gate 12) and hits it with its attack, archers shoot. **Below half** the bricks crack (`bricks_cracked`), dust falls. Health gone — a **breach**: the cells ±2 along the wall through its whole thickness and height become air, half-block rubble on the floor, enemy and hero obstacles are recomputed (`levelChanged`), and all enemies of that road continue the march along `TdLane.inner` to the bush (`Mob.extendMarch`); new ones emerge with the full road right away. Moths (`ghost`) fly along `inner` from the start. A broken gate — the leaves fall, the opening is clear.
- **Repair.** The hero at a wall (within 2.5 m of its rectangle, including standing on it) sees the hint **F — Repair / Rebuild: … N raspberries**: the price — `meta.td.repair` (20) raspberries per missing 100 health, no less than 5; rebuilding a breach entirely — 80. Blocks and leaves return (`Wall.repair`). In the HUD under the bush bar — wall bars (⌂ gate, E, S, W), a breached one — a red frame. **N** repairs everything.
- **Hero outside the walls.** Through the wicket the hero goes out onto the meadow and intercepts groups personally: marching mobs notice the hero within 3.5 m and give chase (leash 10 m), a sapper — only if the hero is closer than 2 m. On the wall the hero is not noticed from below (height difference > 2 m).
- **New enemies** (`src/mobs/td-foes.ts`, nos. 38–39, in `TD_FOES`):
  - **Stump sapper** (`sapper`, 120 health, 1.5 m/s, mass 3) — a walking stump in bark with iron plates and a keg of berry powder on its back. **Armour ×0.3** against any hit except a **piercing** one — a hit without direction (zero `dir` in `Mob.hit`; that is how the wall ballista hits). **Does not notice** the hero (aggro 0, on being hit — only if the hero is within 2 m), walks to the wall, at 2.3 m lights the fuse (1.6 s, the spark blinks faster, the keg shakes) and **explodes**: 160 to the wall, 30 to heroes within 3 m, 40 to its own within 2.5 m. Fell earlier — the powder blows where it fell (`blasted`): kill it far from the wall and close to its own.
  - **Vine ballista** (`ballista`, 140 health, 1.3 m/s, mass 4) — a siege engine on root legs: keeps 9 m from the target and every ~3.4 s fires a heavy bolt in a straight line (`shots`): 24 to gate and walls, 14 to the hero. The gate is its decoy; it stops on the northern road and shoots until it breaks it. Crossbowmen (no. 9) do the same, weaker.
- **New towers** (`src/td/towers.ts`, only where the map allows — `meta.td.towers`; `td.kinds`, keys **6–7**):

| Tower | Price | Range | Reload | Damage | What it fires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wall ballista | 120 | 14 m | 2.2 s | 45 | a heavy bolt in a straight line to the edge of range: everyone in a 0.7 m strip (the first takes full, the rest 70 %), **pierces armour** |
| Tar cauldron | 90 | 6 m | 3.5 s | 7 | a ladle of tar in an arc: a **pool** of radius 2.2 m (+0.3 per level) for 5 s — in it damage every 0.5 s, **−50 % speed** and burning for 2 s |

- **Waves** — `src/td/waves-castle.ts`, ten: scouting at the gate → the first sapper (east) → crossbows at the gate → battering rams from the west → siege ballistas → blowing up the southern wall → moths over the walls → sappers from two sides → ballistas and spores → the great assault (12 sappers from three sides, three ballistas, battering rams, wolves, hammerers, two monsters, moths). ## 16. Match loot and the heroes' strength (done)

Two wallets: the **purse** of the battle (towers, sutler — section 12) and the **loot** — raspberries for the account (`SimTd.loots`, `lootOf`, `addLoot`, `killed`; constants in `src/td/scaling.ts`). Loot is not spent in battle.

- **Earned** by everyone in the party alike: a fallen enemy — its own `loot` (1–5, mini-boss 25; summoned — nothing), a beaten wave — ¼ of its `bounty`, victory — +50 % of everything. All × `lootK = 1 + 0.08·(rating − 1)`.
- **Paid only for victory.** The bush withered, the player left, the tab closed — the loot burns. Solo — `Profile.creditStored` into the stored profile; online — the server (`Battle.rewards`, cap `maxBerries`, [design-multiplayer.md](design-multiplayer.md), section 7). Unspent purse raspberries go nowhere.
- **Strength** (`Profile.rating` = gear power + ⌊(level − 1)/2⌋; a party — the average, `Match.rerate`) is taken from the gear the hero came with — the sutler does not raise it. Each unit above one: +1 pack level on every wave (`SimTd.base`) and +8 % loot. Capped at 40. The camp shows it under the gear power.
- **HUD**: "Loot: N raspberries" under the bush bar, struck out on defeat; the result window — "+N raspberries to the camp" or "Loot lost".
- Tests: `server/test/sim-td.test.ts` (sources of loot, not spent, reset, rating), `rooms.test.ts`/`match.test.ts` (paid on victory, nothing on defeat), `tests/menu.spec.ts` (camp raspberries after defeat and victory).
