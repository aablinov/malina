# Level blocks and the editor

Version 1.5 of 2026-10-31: Crystal Glade blocks — lamp mushrooms and the blue portal (section 5). Version 1.4 of 2026-10-31: lava map blocks (section 5). Version 1.3 of 2026-10-24. Builds on `claude/adr-003-visual-style.md` (Minecraft Dungeons style) and `claude/design-dungeon.md` (rooms as tiles).

## 1. Decision

The world is assembled from a voxel grid and edited in the level editor. The game's only scene is the **playground** (`src/dev/playground.ts`); the garden and its `world.ts` were removed (2026-10-22). A level consists of three layers:

- **data** — `src/level/level.ts`: 16³ chunks, a block index and rotation per cell; maps are stored as **JSON** (`assets/maps/*.json`);
- **palette and shapes** — `src/level/palette.ts` + **`.obj` files** `assets/blocks/*.obj`: a block = a shape + textures in its slots + collision rules;
- **rendering and collisions** — `src/level/mesher.ts` (hybrid chunk mesher) and `src/level/collide.ts` (height-aware collisions).

A full-blown voxel engine (greedy meshing, LOD) is not needed: levels the size of the references are 20–60 thousand blocks, which is 10–30 draw calls. The data model and collisions were rewritten, not the renderer for its own sake.

## 2. Conventions

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Size | 1 block = 1 m. Shapes on a **1/16** grid: half-block 8/16, fence post 4/16, wall 8/16, torch 2/16 |
| Cell | `(x, y, z)` is **centred on integer x, z** and occupies `[x−0.5; x+0.5) × [y; y+1) × [z−0.5; z+0.5)`. The arena floor is the top of the cells at `y = −1`; the fighter stands at `y = 0` |
| Rotation | `rot` 0–3 — quarter turns around Y; `rot = 1` maps +X to +Z |
| Textures | 16 × 16, pixel art, generated by code (`textures.ts`), packed into an atlas with 32 × 32 slots and margins made of stretched edges (mips do not bleed in neighbours) |
| Block names | Maps reference blocks by palette name — names are **never renamed**, only added |
| Collision height | `collide` in the palette: 0 — passable (grass, flowers), 0.5 — half-block and liquid (water and acid are waded: the floor is half a block below the shore), 1 — cube, 1.5 — fence and wall (cannot be stepped over); stairs (`stairs`) are 0.5 over the low half and 1 over the high half — they climb a whole block |
| Hero step | 0.5 m — climbs a half-block or a stair on its own, anything higher is a wall; falls off a ledge |

## 3. Map format (JSON)

```json
{
  "format": "malina-level", "version": 1, "name": "Огород",
  "palette": ["air", "grass", "dirt", "…"],
  "rows": [[y, z, [[x0, length, blockIndex, rotation], …]], …],
  "entities": [],
  "spawn": [0, 0, 2]
}
```

Rows along X with runs of identical blocks: the playground (4.6 thousand blocks) is ~30 KB. `entities` is reserved for spawn points, lanterns and items. Maps from `assets/maps/` are embedded into the build (`import.meta.glob`); the single `dist/index.html` file is preserved.

## 4. Block shapes (.obj)

`assets/blocks/<name>.obj`, a 0..1 cube, only `v / vt / vn / usemtl / f` are needed. `usemtl` is the **texture slot** (`top`, `side`, `bottom`, …); the palette decides which texture goes in the slot. UVs are in fractions of a block, so pixels line up with the grid and a half-block's face takes the bottom half of the texture. A face lying on the cube boundary is tagged with its direction and hidden if the neighbour is opaque.

Shapes are generated by `scripts/gen-blocks.mjs` (`npm run blocks`), but the files can be drawn by hand (Blockbench, Blender) — the main thing is to respect the 0..1 cube and the slots.

| Shape | What it is |
|---|---|
| `cube`, `cube_x` | cube; cube with "end grain" on ±X (a log lying down) |
| `slab`, `stairs`, `layer` | half-block; stairs (rise toward +X, rotate); a thin 2/16 layer |
| `wall_post` + `wall_seg` | an 8/16 wall: post and segments toward neighbours (auto-join) |
| `fence_post` + `fence_rail` | fence: post and two rails toward neighbours |
| `water` | a cube 14/16 high, faces between water are not drawn |
| `cross`, `fire`, `torch`, `torch_flame`, `campfire_logs`, `campfire_fire` | a cross of two planes (grass, flowers, fire); torch; campfire |
| `rail`, `crate` | a garden-bed edge along the cell border; a crate slightly smaller than a cube |

## 6. Rendering

Per 16³ chunk — one mesh per layer: `opaque` and `cutout` (atlas, Lambert, shadows), `water` (own texture, transparent), `fire` (own texture, `MeshBasic`, not tone-mapped → bloom). The boundary between opaque neighbours is not drawn; cube faces get vertex corner shading (AO from three neighbours); brightness wanders from block to block (`jitter`). A chunk is rebuilt on edit (`level.dirty` → `renderer.sync()`).

**Grass on top of blocks** (2026-10-23, after Minecraft Dungeons screenshots — [sources/mcd-lighting.md](sources/mcd-lighting.md)): a block in the palette has `fringe` and `tufts` (currently on grass). The mesher adds on its own:
- **fringe** — a jagged 8/16 strip of grass hanging from the top edge on every side where the neighbour is lower (ledge, shore, half-block); set 1/16 off the face;
- **tufts** — 0–5 grass crosses of 4–7/16 on an open top (air above), denser in patches by smooth noise with a 5 m step, sometimes with flowers; the texture window is chosen so that pixels stay at 16 per metre.

- **ragged edge** between grass and a path (`bleed`): the block's top overlaps the neighbour by 0–8 px by mask — road edges do not follow the cell grid ruler-straight ([style-terrain.md](style-terrain.md)).

All of these are in the `detail` layer: cutout Lambert with the front face only (tufts are emitted in both directions, otherwise the back side flips the normal "up" and darkens), **cast no shadows** and do not enter AO. In the playground — ≈ 58 thousand triangles (blocks — ≈ 30 thousand). The showpiece is the three-tier grass hill by the west wall.

## 7. Height-aware collisions

`collide.ts`: the body is a cylinder (r 0.35, h 1.6) at foot height. `resolve` pushes out of cells whose top is above feet + 0.5; `floorAt` — the floor under a point (the highest block not above feet + 0.5); `wallHit` — where a segment hits a block and the face normal. `collideTop` for stairs depends on the point: 0.5 over the low half, 1 over the high half (taking rotation into account) — so the fighter climbs stairs → a block with a half-block → a platform.

**Fighter** (`hero.ts`, `terrain?`): walls of blocks go through `terrain.resolve` and `floorAt` (stairs, half-blocks, falling), while storage-yard items (barrels, crates) are flat `Obstacle`s. **Enemies and bullets** work with the flat list `obstaclesOf(level, 0, …)` (cells with a top above 0.5, merged into strips along X; height — for bullets) plus `props.solids`; ~~`NavGrid`~~ (removed 2026-11-04, mobs walk via `Pathfinder` — [design-mobs.md](design-mobs.md), section 6) and cover points are built from the same — jailers hide behind block cover, arachnids do not jump over block walls. **Beetles** (`BeetleWorld`) additionally know `resolve`/`floorAt` and an A* path around walls; this will be needed in the dungeon.

## 10. Dungeon rooms and the floor

Templates are ASCII in `src/level/rooms.ts` by the design doc legend: `#` wall, `.` floor, `o` column, `=` low cover (a 14/16 block — cannot be stepped over, can be shot over while standing), `~` acid, `n` burrow, `c` chest, `D` door (a 3-cell opening in the middle of each side), `T` torch, `S` spawn, `M` merchant, `A` altar, `B` boss. `validateRoom` checks the frame and the doors; `roomFromTemplate` builds a `Level`: a slab floor with specks of cobblestone and moss, brick walls 3 blocks high (on the camera side, +X and +Z, — 1 block), doors and items go into `entities`, type and size into `meta.room`. `npm run map:rooms` → `assets/maps/rooms/*.json`; these JSON files can be opened and edited in the editor — the generator takes them, not the ASCII.

Currently 10 templates: start, 4 combat (pillars, acid, hall 24 × 16, cross), elite, storage, merchant, altar, boss.

The floor generator `src/level/dungeon.ts` is deterministic from a seed: a 5 × 5 grid, 32 m step; `layoutFloor` — the main path by random walk of 5–6 rooms (the last one is the boss), 1–2 branches from the elite room, the merchant (mandatory on floors 2–3) or the storage and the altar; `buildFloor` places rooms at the cell centres (the cell centre is at the middle of a door, so corridors between neighbours are straight), bricks up extra doors, lays corridors 3 cells wide with walls and torches, collects `entities` with the room type and cell, `spawn` from the start room. To see it: the "Floor…" button in the editor, then "Play" — the playground on that floor (dungeon mode with waves and doors ahead).

## Hero pathfinding

`src/level/pathfind.ts` — a path by mouse click. A* over grid points at 0.5 m (two per block, so both halves of a stair are visible) at the floor height under the point: upward — no more than a 0.5 m step, downward — up to 3 m, 1.6 m of clearance above the floor; diagonals without corner cutting; nodes next to walls cost more; the path is straightened. Flat obstacles (barrels, crates) are walked around. A long path with climbs takes about 25 ms, an unreachable target (we go to the nearest point) about 40 ms; the path is recomputed only if the target has moved or the hero got stuck.
